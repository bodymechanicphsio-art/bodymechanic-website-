## 2. Hero Rewrite — 2 Variants

**Current:**
> Body Mechanic Physiotherapy Clinic — where expert physiotherapy, hands-on
> rehabilitation, and long-term wellness care come together. We treat the root
> cause of your pain, not just the symptoms, so you can move freely and live
> without limits.

**Problem:** clinic name buried in the middle, no primary keyword in the first
sentence, no location signal, no urgency for the visitor to act.

### Variant A — Warm & question-led *(Recommended for conversion)*

> **Looking for expert physiotherapy in Lahore?** Body Mechanic Physiotherapy
> Clinic treats the real cause of your pain — not just the symptoms — with
> hands-on rehabilitation and personalised care. Whether it's back pain, a
> sports injury, or long-standing stiffness, we help you move freely and live
> without limits.

Primary keyword ("physiotherapy in Lahore") in the first four words. Question
form drives engagement. Three symptom hooks (back pain, sports injury, stiffness)
match real search queries.

### Variant B — Authority-led

> **Body Mechanic Physiotherapy Clinic — Lahore's home for evidence-based
> physiotherapy**, hands-on rehabilitation, and long-lasting wellness care.
> We work with you to find the root cause of your pain and build a treatment
> plan that gets you moving, stronger, and pain-free.

Location + primary keyword in the first sentence. More confident/authoritative
tone. Good for a clinic that already has brand recognition.

### 3 quick technical wins

1. **Schema completeness.** Verify `lib/seo.ts` and `components/ui/JsonLd.tsx`
   emit a full **MedicalBusiness** JSON-LD block for the clinic:
   - `name`, `description`, `url`
   - full `address` (487, J Block, LDA Avenue-1, Lahore, 54000, PK)
   - `geo` with lat/long
   - `telephone: "+923104971086"` (E.164 format — critical for Google)
   - `openingHoursSpecification` array (Mon-Fri 14:00-21:00, Sat 14:00-18:00,
     Sun closed)
   - `sameAs`: Instagram + Google Maps URL + Facebook if any
   - `aggregateRating` (5.0 / 15 reviews)
   - `founder` / `medicalSpecialty` referencing Dr. Saima
   Add a separate **Physician** schema block for Dr. Saima with her
   credentials — massive E-E-A-T win.

2. **NAP consistency audit.** Name / Address / Phone must be **byte-identical**
   across: website (footer, contact, all service pages), Google Business
   Profile, Instagram bio, Facebook page, any listing directories. Even
   variations like `0310-4971086` vs `0310 4971086` vs `+92 310 4971086`
   confuse Google. Pick ONE canonical format (`0310-4971086` for display,
   `+923104971086` for `tel:` and schema) and enforce everywhere. This is
   the single biggest local-SEO win most Lahore clinics miss.

3. **Internal linking with intent.** Each service page should link to:
   - **Two related services** (clinically related, not random) — e.g.
     Sports Rehab → Strength & Mobility + Pain Management.
   - The **About page** (E-E-A-T signal — proves this is a real, credentialed
     clinic).
   - The **Booking page** with the primary keyword in the anchor text where
     natural (e.g. *"book your physiotherapy assessment in Lahore"*).
   Currently the "Related services" cards exist but link generically — audit
   the pairings for clinical relevance.

### Bonus wins (do these after the top 3)

- **Google Business Profile.** Fill every field. Upload 10+ real clinic + team
  photos. Reply to every review with a natural mention of "physiotherapy in
  Lahore" (never spammy — thank first, then a light keyword). Post weekly
  Google Posts (opening hours reminders, service spotlights, patient stories).
- **Localised URL variants.** If `back pain treatment Lahore` shows strong
  volume, consider a dedicated `/back-pain-treatment-lahore` landing page
  that links back into `/services/pain-management`. Same for `sports-injury-rehab-lahore`.
- **hreflang / locale.** Confirm `<html lang="en">` and OpenGraph
  `locale: "en_PK"` (already set in `lib/seo.ts` per earlier audit — verify).
- **Speed.** Compress the 4 remaining 2-MB technique PNGs to under 300 KB via
  TinyPNG. Core Web Vitals directly affect Lahore mobile rankings.
